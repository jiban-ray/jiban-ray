## Current PhD Research

### Trustworthiness Assessment Framework for Distributed AI Systems Based on Reliability, Robustness, and Trust (RRT)

**University of Cádiz, Spain**  
Research collaboration: **Frankfurt University of Applied Sciences, Germany**

My doctoral research aims to develop and experimentally validate a unified framework for assessing the trustworthiness of distributed AI systems through three complementary dimensions: **Reliability, Robustness, and Trust (RRT)**.

The research focuses on measurable indicators, participant-level and system-level assessment, and experimental validation using federated learning under heterogeneous, unreliable, and potentially adversarial operating conditions.

**Key research directions:**

- Identification and evaluation of measurable RRT indicators
- Reliability assessment under participant and communication failures
- Robustness evaluation under heterogeneous and adversarial conditions
- Trust assessment based on participant behavior and available evidence
- Comparative evaluation of trustworthiness assessment approaches
- Reproducible experimental validation in distributed AI environments
